# Fox Corporation (fox)

Fox Corporation is a U.S. media company whose portfolio includes Fox News Media, Fox Sports, Fox Entertainment, Fox Television Stations, and the ad-supported streaming service Tubi. Fox is a Fortune 500 consumer of cloud, AI, and media technology APIs (notably AWS as preferred AI cloud provider) but does not publish a public developer portal at the corporate level.

## APIs

No public developer APIs are documented for Fox Corporation at the corporate level. Internal partner integrations exist for ad sales, distribution, and content syndication across Fox News, Fox Sports, Fox Television Stations, and Tubi, but they are not publicly published.
